# Site: implementatieplan (fase 6)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** De statische pagina uit het siteontwerp: de kaart met twee weergaven en zoom, het blad naast de kaart (de lijst "Drawing effect by state" en het staatsblad), de landelijke strook, de methodetoelichting en de blokken van de uitbreidingen, gevoed door `site/data/results.json` en `site/data/map.topojson`.

**Architecture:** Eén pagina `site/index.html` met één stylesheet `site/style.css` en ES-modules in `site/js/`, zonder bouwstap. d3 en topojson-client komen als globale scripts uit `site/vendor/`. De modules zijn in twee soorten gesplitst. Pure modules (`format.js`, `colors.js`, `hash.js`, `axes.js`, `model.js`, `extensions.js`) maken van `results.json` teksten, kleuren en geometrie, zonder DOM en zonder globale `d3`; elk heeft een testbestand voor `node --test`. DOM-modules (`map.js`, `charts.js`, `sheet.js`, `national.js`, `main.js`) tekenen alleen wat de pure modules opleveren. Een headless controle (`tools/check_site.mjs` met de groepen in `tools/site_checks.mjs`) stuurt Edge of Chrome aan via het DevTools-protocol en controleert per taak het gedrag in de browser. Wat een mens moet zien, staat per taak als lijst.

**Tech Stack:** Node 24 (`node --test`, de ingebouwde `WebSocket` voor de headless controle), d3 7.9.0 en topojson-client 3.1.0 uit `site/vendor/`, Edge of Chrome voor de controle, Python 3.12 uit `.venv` alleen voor `make serve`.

Ontwerp: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-site-design.md` ("het siteontwerp"). Het blok "Bijgewerkt na feedback van de eigenaar op 7 oktober 2026" gaat voor de tekst eronder. Versie 5 van het voorproefje, die de eigenaar goedkeurde, is de referentie voor indeling en gedrag; dit plan neemt de opbouw daarvan over (het bestand `gerrymandering-map-preview.html` stond in de scratchmap van de ontwerpsessie). Hoofdontwerp: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-gerrymandering-map-design.md` ("de spec"), hoofdstuk 7, 8 en 10. Uitbreidingen: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-extensions-design.md` ("het uitbreidingsontwerp"), hoofdstuk 1 tot en met 4 en 7, en `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-extensions.md` ("het uitbreidingsplan", fase 5). ## Keuzes in dit plan

Geen open punten, wel om na te gaan bij de review:

1. **Richting in plaats van teken.** Het bijgewerkte siteontwerp vervangt "+3" en "−6" door "D+3" en "R+6". Dat geldt in dit plan overal waar een effect staat: in het blad, de lijst, het saldostaafje, de legenda van de staten (dertien vakjes met de labels R+6, R+4, R+2, 0, D+2, D+4, D+6, zoals in versie 5; hoofdstuk 3 van het siteontwerp noemde nog −6 tot +6), de landelijke lijst van hertekeningen (hoofdstuk 5 noemde nog "+n" of "−n") en de tabellen per politieke controle. De zin over de hertekening in het blad ("2024 map: 14 Democratic seats with these votes. 2026 map: 11 (3 fewer).") blijft zoals in het uitbreidingsontwerp: die telt zetels, het is geen effect.
2. **Grafieken op maat van hun vak.** Versie 5 tekende elke grafiek in een vaste viewBox van 560 eenheden. In een blad van ongeveer 330 pixels breed werd tekst van 11 eenheden daardoor 6 pixels hoog, en op een breed scherm werd de landelijke as juist twee keer zo groot. Hier is één viewBox-eenheid 1/15 rem (`chartUnits` in `axes.js`): tekst blijft 11 pixels bij een basisgrootte van 15 pixels en groeit mee tot ongeveer 15 pixels bij 21 pixels, zoals het siteontwerp vraagt ("alles schaalt mee"). Een grafiek tekent opnieuw als de breedte van het venster verandert.
3. **Contrast.** Twee waarden uit versie 5 halen de eisen van hoofdstuk 7 niet en zijn aangepast: de staafjes van de zetelas hadden dekking 0,55 (2,4:1 tegen `--sheet`) en krijgen 0,75 (3,5:1); de melding over de kaart ("Use Ctrl + scroll to zoom the map") had een donkere laag van 0,55 (4,35:1 op wit) en krijgt 0,6. Labels bij de markeringen krijgen een rand in `--sheet`, zodat een lijn er niet doorheen loopt. `[hidden]` krijgt `display: none !important`: in versie 5 overschreef `.gesture { display: grid }` het attribuut `hidden`.
4. **Tokens.** De waarden zijn precies die van de tabel in hoofdstuk 3 van het siteontwerp. `--water` en `--band` komen uit versie 5 (het bijgewerkte siteontwerp noemt `--water`), `--focus` is `--ink`. De knop voor een licht of donker thema uit het voorproefje (`data-theme`) vervalt; het siteontwerp vraagt alleen de instelling van het systeem.
5. **Links naar onderdelen.** "National picture", "Method" en de link naar de tabellen per controle zijn gewone ankers (`#national`, `#method`, `#control`). Een anker dat een onderdeel noemt, laat de gekozen staat staan; alleen een staatcode in de link (`#TX`) kiest een staat.
6. **Focus.** Een staat kiezen in de lijst zet de focus op de kop van het blad. Escape of "All states" zet de focus terug op de rij van die staat, of op de kop van de lijst als die rij ingeklapt is. Een klik op de kaart verplaatst de focus niet: op een telefoon staat het blad onder de kaart.
7. **Getallen in de methodetoelichting.** Wat in `results.json` staat (5.000 plannen, 44 staten, 429 en 216 zetels, de ALARM-versie, de datums, het aandeel plannen in Louisiana), vult `main.js` in via `format.js`. Feiten uit hoofdstuk 10 van de spec die niet in `results.json` staan (ongeveer 4.100 stemmen, 17.272 stemmen, 38 staten, acht staten, ongeveer 4.500 stemmen), staan als tekst in `index.html`.
8. **Louisiana.** Hoofdstuk 10 van de spec noemt 99,8%. Volgens de regel van hoofdstuk 8 voor aandelen van plannen (hele procenten, ">99%" onder 100) toont de site ">99%".
9. **De geblokkeerde kaart.** Hoofdstuk 1 van het siteontwerp vraagt voor Missouri de zetels en de positie van HB 1; versie 5 toonde alleen de zetels. Het blad toont beide, met het statuslabel en de regel "Map file:".
10. **Legal status.** Elk staatsblad toont "Legal status as of <datum>" (spec, hoofdstuk 8); versie 5 had die regel niet.
11. **Bronnen.** De alinea "Sources" uit hoofdstuk 9 van het siteontwerp krijgt de licenties die hoofdstuk 3 van de spec vraagt (VEST CC BY 4.0, de census-2020-bestanden van ALARM CC BY-SA 4.0, Census publiek domein). De voet noemt de licenties van D3 en topojson-client. De blokbestanden heten "118th, 119th and 120th Congress": Missouri gebruikt CD118. Taak 6.8 schrijft "118th and 120th"; taak 6.11 voegt "119th" toe, omdat de hertekeningen de kaarten van 2024 uit CD119 gebruiken (uitbreidingsplan, taak 5.1 tot en met 5.3).
12. **Uitbreidingen.** De zin over Proportional onder 50% (open punt 7 van het uitbreidingsplan) staat er alleen als dat aandeel onder 0,5 ligt. De vaste partij van de zes staten met één zetel staat als vaste tekst in het blok over de meerderheid (besluit 6 van het uitbreidingsontwerp). De tabellen per controle noemen elke waarde, ook met 0 staten. De tabellen en de methode staan in één kolom van ongeveer 70 tekens, zoals de schets in hoofdstuk 1 van het siteontwerp.
13. **Staten met één zetel.** Een klik of tik op een van de zes kiest geen staat en verandert de link niet; aanwijzen of tikken toont de vaste tekst "One at-large seat. No districts, so nothing to compare."

```js
/**
 * Efficiency gap or mean-median (fraction, positive favors Republicans) in
 * percentage points with the direction: 0.182 -> "18.2 points toward Republicans".
 * null (mean-median with two districts) -> "Not defined for two districts".
 * @param {number|null} value
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function advantage(value) {
  if (value === null) return 'Not defined for two districts';
  if (typeof value !== 'number' || !Number.isFinite(value)) {
    throw new TypeError(`expected a finite number or null, got ${value}`);
  }
  const points = roundHalfUp(Math.abs(value), 1, 2);
  if (points === '0.0') return 'less than 0.05 points either way';
  return `${points} points toward ${value > 0 ? 'Republicans' : 'Democrats'}`;
}
```

```js
// Effects count Democratic seats (spec chapter 5: positive is more Democratic
// seats). The site shows the direction instead of a sign, so neither party is
// the default (site design, update of October 7, 2026).

function checkWhole(n) {
  if (!Number.isInteger(n)) {
    throw new TypeError(`expected a whole number of seats, got ${n}`);
  }
}

/**
 * A seat effect as a direction: -6 -> "R+6", 3 -> "D+3", 0 -> "0".
 * @param {number} n whole seats, positive toward Democrats
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function effect(n) {
  checkWhole(n);
  if (n === 0) return '0';
  return `${n < 0 ? 'R' : 'D'}+${Math.abs(n)}`;
}

/**
 * A seat effect in words: -6 -> "6 seats toward Republicans",
 * 1 -> "1 seat toward Democrats", 0 -> "no difference".
 * @param {number} n
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function effectWords(n) {
  checkWhole(n);
  if (n === 0) return 'no difference';
  const size = Math.abs(n);
  return `${size} ${size === 1 ? 'seat' : 'seats'} toward ${n < 0 ? 'Republicans' : 'Democrats'}`;
}

/**
 * Notation and words together: -6 -> "R+6 · 6 seats toward Republicans",
 * 0 -> "0 · no difference". The separator is a middle dot (U+00B7).
 * @param {number} n
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function effectLine(n) {
  return `${effect(n)} · ${effectWords(n)}`;
}

/**
 * A whole number with thousands separators: 5000 -> "5,000".
 * @param {number} n
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function count(n) {
  if (!Number.isSafeInteger(n) || n < 0) {
    throw new TypeError(`expected a whole number, got ${n}`);
  }
  return String(n).replace(/\B(?=(\d{3})+(?!\d))/g, ',');
}

/**
 * A fraction as a whole percent, for axis ticks and legends: 0.35 -> "35%".
 * @param {number} fraction
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function percentLabel(fraction) {
  checkFraction(fraction);
  return `${roundHalfUp(fraction, 0, 2)}%`;
}

const MONTHS = ['January', 'February', 'March', 'April', 'May', 'June', 'July',
  'August', 'September', 'October', 'November', 'December'];

/**
 * A date from results.json as text: "2026-10-03" -> "October 3, 2026".
 * @param {string} iso YYYY-MM-DD
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function longDate(iso) {
  const match = /^(\d{4})-(\d{2})-(\d{2})$/.exec(typeof iso === 'string' ? iso : '');
  if (match) {
    const [year, month, day] = match.slice(1).map(Number);
    const date = new Date(Date.UTC(year, month - 1, day));
    if (date.getUTCMonth() === month - 1 && date.getUTCDate() === day) {
      return `${MONTHS[month - 1]} ${day}, ${year}`;
    }
  }
  throw new TypeError(`expected a date as YYYY-MM-DD, got ${iso}`);
}
```

```js
// Color scales for the map, the legends and the charts (site design, chapter 3).
// Pure: the caller passes d3.interpolateLab and the poles, read from the CSS
// tokens, so dark mode and the "Party colors / Neutral colors" toggle only
// swap the poles. node --test runs this module with the vendored d3.
import { effect, percentLabel } from './format.js';

export const SHARE_DOMAIN = [0.2, 0.8]; // district scale; outside it the full pole color
export const SHARE_EXPONENT = 0.85; // as in preview version 5
export const SHARE_TICKS = [0.2, 0.35, 0.5, 0.65, 0.8];
export const EFFECT_MAX = 6; // drawing effect scale in whole seats; beyond 6 the full pole color
export const EFFECT_START = 0.3; // effects start 30% of the way to a pole; each seat adds 70%/6 (preview version 5)

/**
 * The scales for one set of poles.
 * @param {(a: string, b: string) => (t: number) => string} interpolateLab d3.interpolateLab
 * @param {{d: string, r: string, mid: string, blank: string}} poles CSS colors
 */
export function makeColors(interpolateLab, poles) {
  const towardD = interpolateLab(poles.mid, poles.d);
  const towardR = interpolateLab(poles.mid, poles.r);
  const half = (SHARE_DOMAIN[1] - SHARE_DOMAIN[0]) / 2;
  return {
    poles,
    blank: poles.blank,
    /** Fill for a district's Democratic share (fraction), 50% is the midpoint. */
    share(value) {
      const t = Math.max(-1, Math.min(1, (value - 0.5) / half));
      return (t >= 0 ? towardD : towardR)(Math.abs(t) ** SHARE_EXPONENT);
    },
    /** Fill for a drawing effect in whole seats, 0 is the midpoint. */
    effect(seats) {
      if (seats === 0) return towardD(0);
      const t = EFFECT_START + ((1 - EFFECT_START) * Math.min(Math.abs(seats), EFFECT_MAX)) / EFFECT_MAX;
      return (seats > 0 ? towardD : towardR)(t);
    },
    /** Fill for a seat count that goes to one party: the full pole. */
    party(towardDemocrats) {
      return towardDemocrats ? poles.d : poles.r;
    },
  };
}

/** Stops for the district legend's color bar: offset 0 to 1 over 20% to 80%. */
export function shareStops(count = 25) {
  const [low, high] = SHARE_DOMAIN;
  return Array.from({ length: count }, (_, i) => {
    const offset = i / (count - 1);
    return { offset, value: low + (high - low) * offset };
  });
}

/** Labels under the district legend: 20%, 35%, 50%, 65%, 80%, with their offset on the bar. */
export function shareTicks() {
  const [low, high] = SHARE_DOMAIN;
  return SHARE_TICKS.map((value) => ({ offset: (value - low) / (high - low), label: percentLabel(value) }));
}

/** Thirteen boxes from R+6 to D+6; every second one has a label (site design, chapter 3). */
export function effectBoxes() {
  return Array.from({ length: 2 * EFFECT_MAX + 1 }, (_, i) => {
    const value = i - EFFECT_MAX;
    return { value, label: value % 2 === 0 ? effect(value) : null };
  });
}
```

```js
// The chosen state lives in the link as #TX (site design, chapter 4).

/**
 * The state in a location hash, or "" for the whole map.
 * "#TX", "#tx" and "#%54X" give "TX"; a single-seat state, an unknown code,
 * "#method" or a broken escape give "".
 * @param {string} hash location.hash
 * @param {object} states results.json "states", keyed by postal code
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function stateFromHash(hash, states) {
  let code;
  try {
    code = decodeURIComponent(String(hash ?? '').replace(/^#/, '')).trim().toUpperCase();
  } catch {
    return '';
  }
  return /^[A-Z]{2}$/.test(code) && Object.hasOwn(states, code) ? code : '';
}

/**
 * The link for a state: "TX" gives "#TX"; "" gives the page without a hash.
 * @param {string} postal
 * @param {string} path location.pathname + location.search
 * @returns {string}
 */
export function linkFor(postal, path) {
  return postal ? `#${postal}` : path;
}
```

```js
// --- The map (Task 6.2) -------------------------------------------------------------------------

export const MAP = { width: 975, height: 610, maxZoom: 80, fill: 0.88 }; // a chosen state fills 88% of the map

/** Zoom that centers a projected bounding box [[x0, y0], [x1, y1]] and fills 88% of the map: { x, y, k }. */
export function fitTransform([[x0, y0], [x1, y1]], map = MAP) {
  const k = Math.min(map.maxZoom, map.fill / Math.max((x1 - x0) / map.width, (y1 - y0) / map.height));
  return { x: map.width / 2 - (k * (x0 + x1)) / 2, y: map.height / 2 - (k * (y0 + y1)) / 2, k };
}
```

```js
// Headless check of the site in Edge or Chrome over the DevTools protocol, without extra packages.
// The checks themselves are in tools/site_checks.mjs, one group per task of the site plan.
//
//   node tools/check_site.mjs                             serves site/ itself on 127.0.0.1
//   node tools/check_site.mjs --url http://127.0.0.1:8000/   checks a running server (make serve)
//   node tools/check_site.mjs --only map,sheet            runs only these groups
//   node tools/check_site.mjs --shots <folder>            writes full-page screenshots for a person to look at
//
// The browser is the environment variable BROWSER, or else the first of Edge and Chrome in their
// usual places. Exit code 0 when every check passes, 1 otherwise.
import { spawn } from 'node:child_process';
import { createServer } from 'node:http';
import { existsSync, mkdtempSync, mkdirSync, readFileSync, rmSync, writeFileSync } from 'node:fs';
import { tmpdir } from 'node:os';
import { extname, join, normalize, resolve, sep } from 'node:path';
import { fileURLToPath } from 'node:url';
import { GROUPS } from './site_checks.mjs';

const SITE = resolve(fileURLToPath(new URL('../site/', import.meta.url)));
const TYPES = {
  '.html': 'text/html; charset=utf-8', '.js': 'text/javascript; charset=utf-8', '.css': 'text/css; charset=utf-8',
  '.json': 'application/json', '.topojson': 'application/json', '.txt': 'text/plain; charset=utf-8',
};
const BROWSERS = [
  process.env.BROWSER,
  'C:\\Program Files (x86)\\Microsoft\\Edge\\Application\\msedge.exe',
  'C:\\Program Files\\Microsoft\\Edge\\Application\\msedge.exe',
  'C:\\Program Files\\Google\\Chrome\\Application\\chrome.exe',
  '/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome',
  '/Applications/Microsoft Edge.app/Contents/MacOS/Microsoft Edge',
  '/usr/bin/google-chrome', '/usr/bin/chromium', '/usr/bin/microsoft-edge',
].filter(Boolean);
const sleep = (ms) => new Promise((done) => { setTimeout(done, ms); });
const args = process.argv.slice(2);
const option = (name) => { const i = args.indexOf(name); return i >= 0 ? args[i + 1] : undefined; };

/** A static server for site/, so the check needs no other process. */
function serve() {
  const server = createServer((req, res) => {
    const path = decodeURIComponent(new URL(req.url, 'http://localhost').pathname);
    const file = normalize(join(SITE, path.endsWith('/') ? `${path}index.html` : path));
    if (!file.startsWith(SITE + sep) || !existsSync(file)) { res.writeHead(404).end(); return; }
    res.writeHead(200, { 'content-type': TYPES[extname(file)] || 'application/octet-stream' }).end(readFileSync(file));
  });
  return new Promise((done) => { server.listen(0, '127.0.0.1', () => done(server)); });
}

/** Start the browser headless and connect to it. */
async function launch() {
  const exe = BROWSERS.find((path) => existsSync(path));
  if (!exe) throw new Error('No Edge or Chrome found; set BROWSER to the path of one');
  const profile = mkdtempSync(join(tmpdir(), 'site-check-'));
  const child = spawn(exe, ['--headless=new', '--remote-debugging-port=0', `--user-data-dir=${profile}`,
    '--no-first-run', '--no-default-browser-check', '--disable-extensions', 'about:blank'], { stdio: 'ignore' });
  // The browser writes its port to DevToolsActivePort; the file can be locked while it writes.
  let lines = [];
  for (let i = 0; i < 150 && lines.length < 2; i += 1) {
    await sleep(100);
    try { lines = readFileSync(join(profile, 'DevToolsActivePort'), 'utf8').split('\n').filter(Boolean); } catch { lines = []; }
  }
  if (lines.length < 2) throw new Error(`${exe} opened no DevTools port`);
  const ws = new WebSocket(`ws://127.0.0.1:${lines[0]}${lines[1]}`);
  await new Promise((done, fail) => { ws.onopen = done; ws.onerror = fail; });
  const close = async () => {
    ws.close();
    child.kill();
    await sleep(500);
    try { rmSync(profile, { recursive: true, force: true, maxRetries: 10, retryDelay: 200 }); } catch { /* left for the OS */ }
  };
  return { ws, close };
}

/** One tab with its own session; `on` holds the event listeners. */
async function openTab(ws) {
  let next = 1;
  const pending = new Map();
  const on = {};
  ws.onmessage = (message) => {
    const data = JSON.parse(message.data);
    if (data.id && pending.has(data.id)) {
      const { done, fail } = pending.get(data.id);
      pending.delete(data.id);
      if (data.error) fail(new Error(data.error.message)); else done(data.result);
    } else if (data.method && on[data.method]) on[data.method](data.params);
  };
  const send = (method, params = {}, sessionId = undefined) => new Promise((done, fail) => {
    const id = next++;
    pending.set(id, { done, fail });
    ws.send(JSON.stringify({ id, method, params, sessionId }));
  });
  const { targetId } = await send('Target.createTarget', { url: 'about:blank' });
  const { sessionId } = await send('Target.attachToTarget', { targetId, flatten: true });
  const tab = (method, params) => send(method, params, sessionId);
  for (const domain of ['Page', 'Runtime', 'Network', 'Log']) await tab(`${domain}.enable`);
  return { tab, on };
}

async function main() {
  const only = option('--only')?.split(',');
  const shots = option('--shots');
  const server = option('--url') ? null : await serve();
  const base = option('--url') ?? `http://127.0.0.1:${server.address().port}/`;
  const origin = new URL(base).origin;
  const browser = await launch();
  const { tab, on } = await openTab(browser.ws);
  const requests = [];
  const errors = [];
  on['Network.requestWillBeSent'] = (p) => requests.push(p.request.url);
  on['Runtime.consoleAPICalled'] = (p) => { if (p.type === 'error') errors.push(p.args.map((a) => a.value ?? a.description).join(' ')); };
  on['Runtime.exceptionThrown'] = (p) => errors.push(p.exceptionDetails.exception?.description ?? p.exceptionDetails.text);
  on['Log.entryAdded'] = (p) => { if (p.entry.level === 'error') errors.push(`${p.entry.text} ${p.entry.url ?? ''}`); };

  let failed = 0;
  let total = 0;
  const js = async (expression) => {
    const r = await tab('Runtime.evaluate', { expression, awaitPromise: true, returnByValue: true });
    if (r.exceptionDetails) throw new Error(`${expression}\n${r.exceptionDetails.exception?.description ?? r.exceptionDetails.text}`);
    return r.result.value;
  };
  const t = {
    sleep,
    js,
    check(name, ok, detail = '') {
      total += 1;
      if (!ok) failed += 1;
      console.log(`${ok ? 'PASS' : 'FAIL'}  ${name}${detail ? `  (${detail})` : ''}`);
    },
    async until(expression, ms = 10000) {
      for (let waited = 0; waited < ms; waited += 100) {
        if (await js(expression)) return true;
        await sleep(100);
      }
      return false;
    },
    text: (selector) => js(`document.querySelector(${JSON.stringify(selector)})?.textContent ?? ''`),
    /** Open the page at `hash` with a window size and media features; waits until the map is drawn. */
    async load(hash = '', { width = 1280, height = 800, dark = false, reduce = false } = {}) {
      await tab('Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride', { width, height, deviceScaleFactor: 1, mobile: width < 768 });
      await tab('Emulation.setEmulatedMedia', { features: [
        { name: 'prefers-color-scheme', value: dark ? 'dark' : 'light' },
        { name: 'prefers-reduced-motion', value: reduce ? 'reduce' : 'no-preference' },
      ] });
      await tab('Page.navigate', { url: `${base}${hash}` });
      await sleep(300);
      return this.until("document.querySelectorAll('#map path.district').length > 0 && document.querySelector('#map > g') !== null");
    },
    /** Viewport point at the center of the element that `expression` returns, after scrolling it into view. */
    center: (expression) => js(`(() => { const el = ${expression}; el.scrollIntoView({ block: 'center' });
      const r = el.getBoundingClientRect(); return { x: r.x + r.width / 2, y: r.y + r.height / 2 }; })()`),
    mouse: (type, { x, y }, extra = {}) => tab('Input.dispatchMouseEvent', { type, x, y, ...extra }),
    async click(point) {
      await this.mouse('mousePressed', point, { button: 'left', clickCount: 1 });
      await this.mouse('mouseReleased', point, { button: 'left', clickCount: 1 });
    },
    async key(key, code, text = undefined) {
      await tab('Input.dispatchKeyEvent', { type: 'keyDown', key, code: key, windowsVirtualKeyCode: code, text });
      await tab('Input.dispatchKeyEvent', { type: 'keyUp', key, code: key, windowsVirtualKeyCode: code });
    },
    async screenshot(name) {
      if (!shots) return;
      mkdirSync(shots, { recursive: true });
      const { cssContentSize: size } = await tab('Page.getLayoutMetrics');
      const { data } = await tab('Page.captureScreenshot', { format: 'png', captureBeyondViewport: true,
        clip: { x: 0, y: 0, width: size.width, height: size.height, scale: 1 } });
      writeFileSync(join(shots, `${name}.png`), Buffer.from(data, 'base64'));
    },
  };

  try {
    for (const group of GROUPS) {
      if (only && !only.includes(group.name)) continue;
      console.log(`-- ${group.name}`);
      await group.run(t);
    }
    console.log('-- requests and console');
    const foreign = requests.filter((url) => !url.startsWith(origin) && !url.startsWith('data:') && url !== 'about:blank');
    t.check('no requests to other hosts', foreign.length === 0, foreign.join(' '));
    t.check('no errors in the console', errors.length === 0, errors.join(' | '));
  } finally {
    await browser.close();
    server?.close();
  }
  console.log(`\n${total} checks, ${failed} failed`);
  process.exit(failed ? 1 : 0);
}

main().catch((error) => { console.error(error); process.exit(1); });
```

```js
// The map: two views, zoom, tooltips and the tap line (site design, chapter 4 and the
// update of October 7, 2026). Ctrl or Cmd plus scroll zooms, a plain scroll scrolls the
// page; on a touch screen two fingers zoom and move, one finger scrolls the page.
import { MAP, fitTransform } from './axes.js';
import { shareStops, shareTicks, effectBoxes } from './colors.js';
import { districtTip, stateTip, tapLine, LEGEND_TEXT } from './model.js';

const { d3, topojson } = globalThis;
const ZOOM_MS = 650;

/**
 * @param {object} o
 * @param {SVGSVGElement} o.svg     the map (viewBox 0 0 975 610)
 * @param {HTMLElement} o.box       the box around the map, for the place of the tooltip
 * @param {HTMLElement} o.tip       the tooltip
 * @param {HTMLElement} o.tapline   the line under the map
 * @param {HTMLElement} o.gesture   the message over the map
 * @param {object} o.index          indexResults(results)
 * @param {object} o.topology       map.topojson
 * @param {object} o.hints          mapHints(...)
 * @param {(postal: string) => void} o.onSelect   called for a state with districts
 * @param {() => boolean} o.reduceMotion
 */
export function createMap({ svg, box, tip, tapline, gesture, index, topology, hints, onSelect, reduceMotion }) {
  const statesGeo = topojson.feature(topology, topology.objects.states);
  const districtsGeo = topojson.feature(topology, topology.objects.districts);
  const projection = d3.geoAlbersUsa().fitExtent([[8, 8], [MAP.width - 8, MAP.height - 8]], statesGeo);
  const path = d3.geoPath(projection);
  const root = d3.select(svg);
  root.append('rect').attr('class', 'bg').attr('width', MAP.width).attr('height', MAP.height);
  const layer = root.append('g');
  const districtLayer = layer.append('g');
  const stateLayer = layer.append('g');
  const borderLayer = layer.append('g');
  let view = 'districts';

  function showTip(event, content) {
    const title = Object.assign(document.createElement('b'), { textContent: content.title });
    const lines = content.lines.map((line) => Object.assign(document.createElement('span'), { textContent: line }));
    tip.replaceChildren(title, ...lines);
    tip.hidden = false;
    const frame = box.getBoundingClientRect();
    let x = event.clientX - frame.left + 12;
    const y = event.clientY - frame.top + 12;
    if (x + tip.offsetWidth > frame.width) x = Math.max(4, event.clientX - frame.left - tip.offsetWidth - 12);
    tip.style.left = `${x}px`;
    tip.style.top = `${y}px`;
  }
  const hideTip = () => { tip.hidden = true; };

  function choose(postal, districtId) {
    tapline.textContent = tapLine(index, districtId, postal, view);
    if (index.states[postal]) onSelect(postal);
  }

  const districts = districtLayer.selectAll('path').data(districtsGeo.features).join('path')
    .attr('class', 'district').attr('d', path)
    .on('mousemove', (event, f) => showTip(event, districtTip(index, f.properties.id, f.properties.state)))
    .on('mouseleave', hideTip)
    .on('click', (event, f) => choose(f.properties.state, f.properties.id));
  const states = stateLayer.selectAll('path').data(statesGeo.features).join('path')
    .attr('class', 'statefill').attr('d', path)
    .on('mousemove', (event, f) => showTip(event, stateTip(index, f.properties.postal)))
    .on('mouseleave', hideTip)
    .on('click', (event, f) => choose(f.properties.postal, null));
  const borders = borderLayer.selectAll('path').data(statesGeo.features).join('path')
    .attr('class', 'state').attr('d', path);

  // Zoom: wheel only with Ctrl or Cmd, touch only with two fingers, drag with the mouse.
  const zoom = d3.zoom()
    .scaleExtent([1, MAP.maxZoom])
    .translateExtent([[0, 0], [MAP.width, MAP.height]])
    .filter((event) => {
      if (event.type === 'wheel') return event.ctrlKey || event.metaKey;
      if (event.type === 'touchstart') return event.touches.length > 1;
      return !event.button;
    })
    .on('start', (event) => { if (event.sourceEvent && event.sourceEvent.type === 'mousedown') svg.classList.add('dragging'); })
    .on('end', () => svg.classList.remove('dragging'))
    .on('zoom', (event) => layer.attr('transform', event.transform));
  root.call(zoom).on('dblclick.zoom', null);

  let timer;
  function flash(message) {
    gesture.textContent = message;
    gesture.hidden = false;
    clearTimeout(timer);
    timer = setTimeout(() => { gesture.hidden = true; }, 1300);
  }
  svg.addEventListener('wheel', (event) => { if (!event.ctrlKey && !event.metaKey) flash(hints.wheel); }, { passive: true });
  let start = null;
  svg.addEventListener('touchstart', (event) => { start = event.touches.length === 1 ? event.touches[0].clientX : null; }, { passive: true });
  svg.addEventListener('touchmove', (event) => {
    if (start !== null && event.touches.length === 1 && Math.abs(event.touches[0].clientX - start) > 12) {
      flash(hints.touch);
      start = null;
    }
  }, { passive: true });

  const move = (animate) => root.transition().duration(animate && !reduceMotion() ? ZOOM_MS : 0);
  return {
    /** Fill the districts or the states with the current colors and outline the chosen state. */
    paint(colors, nextView, selected) {
      view = nextView;
      districts.attr('fill', (f) => (index.single[f.properties.state] ? colors.blank : colors.share(index.shareOf[f.properties.id])));
      states.attr('fill', (f) => (index.single[f.properties.postal] ? colors.blank : colors.effect(index.states[f.properties.postal].effects.drawing)));
      districtLayer.attr('display', view === 'districts' ? null : 'none');
      stateLayer.attr('display', view === 'states' ? null : 'none');
      borders.classed('on', (f) => f.properties.postal === selected);
      borders.filter((f) => f.properties.postal === selected).raise();
    },
    /** Zoom to a state, or back to the whole map for "". */
    zoomTo(postal, animate) {
      const feature = statesGeo.features.find((f) => f.properties.postal === postal);
      if (!feature) {
        move(animate).call(zoom.transform, d3.zoomIdentity);
        return;
      }
      const t = fitTransform(path.bounds(feature));
      move(animate).call(zoom.transform, d3.zoomIdentity.translate(t.x, t.y).scale(t.k));
    },
    zoomBy(factor) {
      move(true).call(zoom.scaleBy, factor);
    },
  };
}

/** The legend under the map for the current view (site design, chapter 3). */
export function drawLegend(el, view, colors) {
  const legend = d3.select(el).html('');
  const width = 260;
  const svg = legend.append('svg').attr('viewBox', `0 0 ${width} 44`).attr('aria-hidden', 'true');
  if (view === 'districts') {
    const gradient = svg.append('defs').append('linearGradient').attr('id', 'legend-share');
    for (const stop of shareStops()) gradient.append('stop').attr('offset', stop.offset).attr('stop-color', colors.share(stop.value));
    svg.append('rect').attr('x', 10).attr('y', 4).attr('width', width - 20).attr('height', 12).attr('fill', 'url(#legend-share)');
    for (const tick of shareTicks()) {
      svg.append('text').attr('x', 10 + tick.offset * (width - 20)).attr('y', 28).attr('text-anchor', 'middle').text(tick.label);
    }
    svg.append('text').attr('x', 10).attr('y', 41).text(LEGEND_TEXT.low);
    svg.append('text').attr('x', width - 10).attr('y', 41).attr('text-anchor', 'end').text(LEGEND_TEXT.high);
    legend.append('span').text(`${LEGEND_TEXT.districts} ${LEGEND_TEXT.blank}`);
  } else {
    const boxes = effectBoxes();
    const step = (width - 20) / boxes.length;
    boxes.forEach((b, i) => {
      svg.append('rect').attr('x', 10 + i * step).attr('y', 4).attr('width', step - 1).attr('height', 12).attr('fill', colors.effect(b.value));
      if (b.label) svg.append('text').attr('x', 10 + i * step + step / 2).attr('y', 28).attr('text-anchor', 'middle').text(b.label);
    });
    legend.append('span').text(`${LEGEND_TEXT.states} ${LEGEND_TEXT.blank}`);
  }
}
```

```js
// Entry point: loads site/data/results.json and site/data/map.topojson, then wires the map,
// the sheet beside it and the national strip. d3 and topojson come from site/vendor/ as
// globals; the page makes no other requests.
import { stateFromHash, linkFor } from './hash.js';
import { makeColors } from './colors.js';
import { indexResults, stateOptions, mapHints } from './model.js';
import { createMap, drawLegend } from './map.js';

const { d3 } = globalThis;
const $ = (id) => document.getElementById(id);
const reduceMotion = window.matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)');
const darkMode = window.matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: dark)');

async function getJson(url) {
  const response = await fetch(url);
  if (!response.ok) throw new Error(`${url} answered ${response.status}`);
  return response.json();
}

/** The two poles of the current palette, read from the CSS tokens (light or dark). */
function readPoles(palette) {
  const css = getComputedStyle(document.documentElement);
  const token = (name) => css.getPropertyValue(name).trim();
  return {
    d: token(palette === 'party' ? '--party-d' : '--neutral-d'),
    r: token(palette === 'party' ? '--party-r' : '--neutral-r'),
    mid: token('--mid'),
    blank: token('--blank'),
  };
}

async function main() {
  let results;
  let topology;
  try {
    [results, topology] = await Promise.all([getJson('data/results.json'), getJson('data/map.topojson')]);
  } catch (error) {
    $('load-error').textContent = `The map data did not load (${error.message}). `
      + 'Open the page through a web server, for example with make serve, not as a file.';
    $('load-error').hidden = false;
    return;
  }

  const index = indexResults(results);
  const ui = { view: 'districts', palette: 'party', selected: '' };
  let colors = makeColors(d3.interpolateLab, readPoles(ui.palette));
  const hints = mapHints({
    touch: window.matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)').matches,
    mac: /Mac|iPhone|iPad/.test(navigator.platform || navigator.userAgent),
  });
  $('maphint').textContent = hints.hint;

  const map = createMap({
    svg: $('map'), box: $('mapbox'), tip: $('tip'), tapline: $('tapline'), gesture: $('gesture'),
    index, topology, hints,
    onSelect: (postal) => select(postal, { push: true }),
    reduceMotion: () => reduceMotion.matches,
  });

  const pick = $('pick');
  for (const option of stateOptions(results)) pick.append(new Option(option.name, option.postal));
  pick.addEventListener('change', () => select(pick.value, { push: true }));

  function paint() {
    map.paint(colors, ui.view, ui.selected);
    drawLegend($('legend'), ui.view, colors);
  }

  /** Choose a state ("" for the whole map): outline, zoom and the link (#TX). */
  function select(postal, { push = false, animate = true } = {}) {
    const previous = ui.selected;
    ui.selected = postal;
    pick.value = postal;
    if (push && postal !== previous) history.pushState(null, '', linkFor(postal, location.pathname + location.search));
    paint();
    if (postal !== previous) map.zoomTo(postal, animate);
  }

  function fromLocation() {
    const postal = stateFromHash(location.hash, results.states);
    const target = location.hash.slice(1);
    if (!postal && target && document.getElementById(target)) return; // a link to a section, such as #method
    select(postal);
  }
  window.addEventListener('popstate', fromLocation);
  window.addEventListener('hashchange', fromLocation);

  const pressed = (on, off) => { $(on).setAttribute('aria-pressed', 'true'); $(off).setAttribute('aria-pressed', 'false'); };
  $('view-districts').addEventListener('click', () => { ui.view = 'districts'; pressed('view-districts', 'view-states'); paint(); });
  $('view-states').addEventListener('click', () => { ui.view = 'states'; pressed('view-states', 'view-districts'); paint(); });
  const setPalette = (palette) => {
    ui.palette = palette;
    colors = makeColors(d3.interpolateLab, readPoles(palette));
    paint();
  };
  $('palette-party').addEventListener('click', () => { pressed('palette-party', 'palette-neutral'); setPalette('party'); });
  $('palette-neutral').addEventListener('click', () => { pressed('palette-neutral', 'palette-party'); setPalette('neutral'); });
  darkMode.addEventListener('change', () => setPalette(ui.palette));
  $('zoom-in').addEventListener('click', () => map.zoomBy(1.8));
  $('zoom-out').addEventListener('click', () => map.zoomBy(1 / 1.8));
  $('zoom-reset').addEventListener('click', () => { select('', { push: true }); map.zoomTo('', true); });

  select(stateFromHash(location.hash, results.states), { animate: false });
}

main();
```

```js
export const STATUS_LABELS = {
  'In effect': 'In effect', Challenged: 'Challenged in court', Blocked: 'Blocked', 'At-large': 'At-large',
};
const sum = (values) => values.reduce((a, b) => a + b, 0);
const plural = (n, one, many) => (n === 1 ? one : many);

/** The list without a chosen state: states toward Republicans, zero states, states toward Democrats, and the sums. */
export function effectList(results) {
  const rows = Object.entries(results.states)
    .map(([postal, s]) => ({ postal, name: s.name, drawing: s.effects.drawing, label: effect(s.effects.drawing) }))
    .sort((a, b) => a.drawing - b.drawing || a.name.localeCompare(b.name, 'en'));
  const toR = rows.filter((r) => r.drawing < 0);
  const zero = rows.filter((r) => r.drawing === 0);
  const toD = rows.filter((r) => r.drawing > 0);
  const seatsR = -sum(toR.map((r) => r.drawing));
  const seatsD = sum(toD.map((r) => r.drawing));
  const net = seatsD - seatsR;
  const moves = (n, total, party) => (n === 0
    ? `no state moves a seat toward ${party}`
    : `${count(n)} ${plural(n, 'state moves', 'states move')} ${seats(total)} ${plural(total, 'seat', 'seats')} toward ${party}`);
  const first = moves(toR.length, seatsR, 'Republicans');
  return {
    toR, zero, toD, seatsR, seatsD, net,
    zeroLabel: `${count(zero.length)} ${plural(zero.length, 'state matches its', 'states match their')} neutral median`,
    summary: `${first[0].toUpperCase()}${first.slice(1)}, ${moves(toD.length, seatsD, 'Democrats')}. Net: ${effectWords(net)}.`,
    bar: {
      left: `${seats(seatsR)} toward Republicans`,
      right: `${seats(seatsD)} toward Democrats`,
      net: `Net ${effect(net)}`,
      label: `${seats(seatsR)} seats toward Republicans, ${seats(seatsD)} toward Democrats, net ${effectWords(net)}`,
    },
  };
}

function bandText(neutral, unit) {
  return neutral.p5 === neutral.p95
    ? `90% of ${unit}: ${seats(neutral.p5)}`
    : `90% of ${unit}: ${seats(neutral.p5)} to ${seats(neutral.p95)}`;
}

/** "Of the 5,000 neutral maps, 0% give fewer Democratic seats than the enacted map, 0% the same number and 100% more." */
export function positionText(position, plans, what) {
  return `Of the ${count(plans)} neutral maps, ${planShare(position.fewer)} give fewer Democratic seats than ${what}, `
    + `${planShare(position.equal)} the same number and ${planShare(position.more)} more.`;
}

function measureContext(m) {
  return `(neutral maps: ${advantage(m.median)}; 90% between ${advantage(m.p5)} and ${advantage(m.p95)})`;
}

function axisLabel(name, a, unit) {
  const band = a.p5 === a.p95 ? seats(a.p5) : `${seats(a.p5)} to ${seats(a.p95)}`;
  return `Democratic seats in ${name}: proportional ${seats(a.proportional)}, neutral baseline ${seats(a.median)} `
    + `(90% of ${unit} ${band}), enacted ${seats(a.enacted)}.`;
}

export function stateSheet(results, postal) {
  const s = results.states[postal];
  const plans = sum(s.neutral.dist);
  const axis = {
    lo: 0, hi: s.seats, dist: s.neutral.dist, proportional: s.proportional,
    median: s.neutral.median, p5: s.neutral.p5, p95: s.neutral.p95, enacted: s.enacted,
  };
  const blocked = s.blocked_map && {
    name: s.blocked_map.name,
    status: STATUS_LABELS[s.blocked_map.status],
    seatsText: `With these votes it would give ${seats(s.blocked_map.enacted)} Democratic ${plural(s.blocked_map.enacted, 'seat', 'seats')}.`,
    position: positionText(s.blocked_map.position, plans, s.blocked_map.name),
    source: s.blocked_map.source,
  };
  const shares = s.sorted.enacted;
  return {
    postal,
    name: s.name,
    mapName: s.map_name,
    status: STATUS_LABELS[s.status],
    legal: `Legal status as of ${longDate(results.meta.status_checked)}`,
    seatCount: `${seats(s.seats)} seats`,
    trio: [
      { label: 'Proportional', value: seats(s.proportional), sub: `${share(s.d_share)} Democratic vote` },
      { label: 'Neutral baseline', value: seats(s.neutral.median), sub: bandText(s.neutral, 'maps') },
      { label: 'Enacted', value: seats(s.enacted), sub: s.outside_band ? 'outside the neutral band' : 'inside the neutral band' },
    ],
    seatsOf: `Democratic seats of ${seats(s.seats)}.`,
    axis,
    axisLabel: `${axisLabel(s.name, axis, 'maps')} Bars: share of the ${count(plans)} neutral maps per seat count.`,
    effects: [
      { label: 'Geographic effect', value: effectLine(s.effects.geographic), note: '(neutral maps compared with proportional)' },
      { label: 'Drawing effect', value: effectLine(s.effects.drawing), note: '(enacted map compared with neutral maps)' },
    ],
    position: positionText(s.position, plans, 'the enacted map'),
    blocked: blocked || null,
    sorted: s.sorted,
    sortedLabel: `${s.name}: the ${count(s.seats)} enacted districts from lowest to highest Democratic share, `
```

```js
// --- The charts (Task 6.5) ----------------------------------------------------------------------

export const BASE_FONT = 15; // px; chart text is 11 units, so 11px at this root size

/**
 * Width of a chart in viewBox units for a box of `containerPx` CSS pixels. One unit is
 * 1/15 rem, so chart text grows with the page from 15px to 21px (site design, update of
 * October 7) and does not shrink in a narrow sheet; between `min` and `max` units.
 */
export function chartUnits(containerPx, rootPx, max, min = 280) {
  return Math.round(Math.max(min, Math.min(max, (containerPx * BASE_FONT) / rootPx)));
}

export const SEAT_AXIS = { width: 560, height: 124, margin: { l: 14, r: 14, t: 40, b: 22 } };
export const NATIONAL_PAD = 14; // the national axis runs 14 seats below and above the band
export const LABEL_GAP = 92; // labels closer than this (in viewBox units) share no row
export const LABEL_ROWS = 3; // three markers can meet: Proportional, Neutral, Enacted

/** Domain of the national seat axis: 14 seats around the band, wide enough for all three markers. */
export function nationalSeatDomain(axis, seatsTotal, pad = NATIONAL_PAD) {
  const values = [axis.p5 - pad, axis.p95 + pad, axis.proportional, axis.enacted, axis.median];
  return [Math.max(0, Math.min(...values)), Math.min(seatsTotal, Math.max(...values))];
}

/**
 * Label rows for markers on one axis. Row 0 sits right above the plot; a label
 * that would collide with one already in a row moves one row up (site design,
 * chapter 5). Labels near an edge are anchored to stay inside the chart.
 * @param {{key: string, x: number}[]} marks in drawing order
 * @returns {{key: string, x: number, row: number, anchor: string}[]}
 */
export function placeLabels(marks, width = SEAT_AXIS.width, gap = LABEL_GAP, rows = LABEL_ROWS) {
  const placed = [];
  const order = marks.map((m, i) => ({ ...m, i })).sort((a, b) => a.x - b.x || a.i - b.i);
  for (const mark of order) {
    let row = 0;
    while (row < rows - 1 && placed.some((p) => p.row === row && Math.abs(p.x - mark.x) < gap)) row += 1;
    const anchor = mark.x < 70 ? 'start' : mark.x > width - 70 ? 'end' : 'middle';
    placed.push({ key: mark.key, x: mark.x, row, anchor });
  }
  return placed;
}

/** Vertical domain of the sorted district chart: at least 20% to 80%, plus a margin of 2 points. */
export function sortedDomain(sorted) {
  const low = Math.min(0.2, ...sorted.p5, ...sorted.enacted) - 0.02;
  const high = Math.max(0.8, ...sorted.p95, ...sorted.enacted) + 0.02;
  return [Math.max(0, low), Math.min(1, high)];
}

/** The net bar under the list: seats toward Republicans to the left, toward Democrats to the right. */
export function netBar(seatsR, seatsD, width = 360) {
  const center = width / 2;
  const scale = (center - 20) / Math.max(seatsR, seatsD, 1);
  const net = seatsD - seatsR;
  const netX = center + net * scale;
  return {
    center,
    left: { x: center - seatsR * scale, width: seatsR * scale },
    right: { x: center, width: seatsD * scale },
    netX,
    anchor: netX < 60 ? 'start' : netX > width - 60 ? 'end' : 'middle',
  };
}
```

```js
// The charts (site design, chapter 5): the seat axis, the sorted districts and the net bar.
// Geometry comes from axes.js, numbers from format.js; this module only draws.
import { BASE_FONT, SEAT_AXIS, LABEL_GAP, chartUnits, placeLabels, sortedDomain, netBar } from './axes.js';
import { seats, percentLabel } from './format.js';

const { d3 } = globalThis;

/** An empty svg in `el`, sized so that one viewBox unit is 1/15 rem; returns it with its width in units. */
function chartSvg(el, maxUnits, height, label) {
  const rootPx = Number.parseFloat(getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).fontSize) || BASE_FONT;
  const width = chartUnits(el.getBoundingClientRect().width, rootPx, maxUnits);
  const svg = d3.select(el).html('').append('svg')
    .attr('viewBox', `0 0 ${width} ${height}`).style('max-width', `${width / BASE_FONT}rem`)
    .attr('class', 'chart').attr('role', 'img').attr('aria-label', label);
  return { svg, width };
}

/**
 * Markers with labels: Proportional dashed with a square, Neutral a solid line, Enacted a dot,
 * and "base", the actual vote on the vote axis (Task 6.10), a dotted line.
 */
function drawMarks(svg, marks, width, plotBottom, top, rows) {
  const text = Object.fromEntries(marks.map((mark) => [mark.key, mark.text]));
  const placed = placeLabels(marks, width, LABEL_GAP, rows);
  const labelY = (p) => top - 6 - 12 * p.row; // row 0 right above the plot, each next row 12 units higher
  for (const p of placed) {
    svg.append('line').attr('x1', p.x).attr('x2', p.x).attr('y1', labelY(p) + 3).attr('y2', plotBottom)
      .attr('stroke', p.key === 'base' ? 'var(--muted)' : 'var(--fg)')
      .attr('stroke-width', p.key === 'neutral' ? 2 : 1)
      .attr('stroke-dasharray', { proportional: '3 2', base: '1 2' }[p.key] ?? null);
    if (p.key === 'enacted') svg.append('circle').attr('cx', p.x).attr('cy', plotBottom - 6).attr('r', 5).attr('fill', 'var(--fg)');
    if (p.key === 'proportional') {
      svg.append('rect').attr('x', p.x - 4).attr('y', plotBottom - 10).attr('width', 8).attr('height', 8)
        .attr('fill', 'var(--sheet)').attr('stroke', 'var(--fg)');
    }
  }
  for (const p of placed) { // labels last, so their halo covers the lines
    svg.append('text').attr('class', 'mark-label').attr('x', p.x).attr('y', labelY(p)).attr('text-anchor', p.anchor).text(text[p.key]);
  }
}

/**
 * Seat axis: bars for the share of neutral plans per seat count, the 5th to 95th
 * percentile band, and the markers for Proportional, Neutral and Enacted.
 * @param {Element} el
 * @param {{dist: number[], proportional: number, median: number, p5: number, p95: number, enacted: number}} axis
 * @param {[number, number]} domain whole seats shown, lo to hi
 * @param {string} label text alternative
 * @param {number} maxUnits widest chart in viewBox units
 */
export function drawSeatAxis(el, axis, [lo, hi], label, maxUnits = SEAT_AXIS.width) {
  const { height: H, margin: m } = SEAT_AXIS;
  const { svg, width: W } = chartSvg(el, maxUnits, H, label);
  const x = d3.scaleLinear().domain([lo - 0.5, hi + 0.5]).range([m.l, W - m.r]);
  const total = d3.sum(axis.dist);
  const shown = axis.dist.slice(lo, hi + 1).map((n, i) => [lo + i, n / total]);
  const y = d3.scaleLinear().domain([0, d3.max(shown, (d) => d[1]) || 1]).range([H - m.b, m.t + 8]);
  svg.append('rect').attr('x', x(axis.p5 - 0.5)).attr('y', m.t + 4)
    .attr('width', x(axis.p95 + 0.5) - x(axis.p5 - 0.5)).attr('height', H - m.b - m.t - 4).attr('fill', 'var(--band)');
  const barWidth = Math.max(1, Math.min(18, (x(1) - x(0)) * 0.8));
  svg.append('g').selectAll('rect').data(shown).join('rect').attr('class', 'bar')
    .attr('x', (d) => x(d[0]) - barWidth / 2).attr('width', barWidth)
    .attr('y', (d) => y(d[1])).attr('height', (d) => H - m.b - y(d[1]));
  const ticks = x.ticks(Math.min(10, hi - lo + 1, Math.floor(W / 40))).filter(Number.isInteger);
  svg.append('g').attr('class', 'axis').attr('transform', `translate(0,${H - m.b})`)
    .call(d3.axisBottom(x).tickValues(ticks).tickSize(3).tickFormat((d) => seats(d)));
  drawMarks(svg, [
    { key: 'proportional', x: x(axis.proportional), text: `Proportional ${seats(axis.proportional)}` },
    { key: 'neutral', x: x(axis.median), text: `Neutral ${seats(axis.median)}` },
    { key: 'enacted', x: x(axis.enacted), text: `Enacted ${seats(axis.enacted)}` },
  ], W, H - m.b, m.t, 3);
}

/** Sorted districts: enacted shares as dots against the neutral band and median per position, a dashed line at 50%. */
export function drawSorted(el, sorted, colors, label) {
  const H = 190;
  const m = { l: 34, r: 10, t: 10, b: 24 };
  const { svg, width: W } = chartSvg(el, 560, H, label);
  const n = sorted.enacted.length;
  const x = d3.scaleLinear().domain([0.5, n + 0.5]).range([m.l, W - m.r]);
  const y = d3.scaleLinear().domain(sortedDomain(sorted)).range([H - m.b, m.t]);
  const index = d3.range(n);
  const area = d3.area().x((i) => x(i + 1)).y0((i) => y(sorted.p5[i])).y1((i) => y(sorted.p95[i])).curve(d3.curveStep);
  svg.append('path').attr('d', area(index)).attr('fill', 'var(--band)');
  svg.append('line').attr('x1', m.l).attr('x2', W - m.r).attr('y1', y(0.5)).attr('y2', y(0.5))
    .attr('stroke', 'var(--muted)').attr('stroke-dasharray', '4 3');
  svg.append('text').attr('x', W - m.r).attr('y', y(0.5) - 4).attr('text-anchor', 'end').text(percentLabel(0.5));
  svg.append('path').attr('d', d3.line().x((i) => x(i + 1)).y((i) => y(sorted.p50[i])).curve(d3.curveStep)(index))
    .attr('fill', 'none').attr('stroke', 'var(--muted)').attr('stroke-width', 1.5);
  svg.append('g').selectAll('circle').data(sorted.enacted).join('circle')
    .attr('cx', (v, i) => x(i + 1)).attr('cy', (v) => y(v)).attr('r', n > 30 ? 3 : 4)
    .attr('fill', (v) => colors.party(v > 0.5)).attr('stroke', 'var(--sheet)').attr('stroke-width', 1);
  svg.append('g').attr('class', 'axis').attr('transform', `translate(${m.l},0)`)
    .call(d3.axisLeft(y).ticks(5).tickSize(3).tickFormat((v) => percentLabel(v)));
  svg.append('text').attr('x', m.l).attr('y', H - 6).text('Districts from lowest to highest Democratic share');
}
```

```js
// --- contrast (Task 6.9) ---

// WCAG 2 relative luminance and contrast ratio
const channel = (v) => { const s = v / 255; return s <= 0.04045 ? s / 12.92 : ((s + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4; };
const rgb = (hex) => [1, 3, 5].map((i) => Number.parseInt(hex.slice(i, i + 2), 16));
const luminance = (hex) => { const [r, g, b] = rgb(hex).map(channel); return 0.2126 * r + 0.7152 * g + 0.0722 * b; };
const contrast = (a, b) => { const [hi, lo] = [luminance(a), luminance(b)].sort((x, y) => y - x); return (hi + 0.05) / (lo + 0.05); };
/** `top` with opacity `alpha` over `bottom`, as hex */
const blend = (top, bottom, alpha) => `#${rgb(top).map((v, i) => Math.round(alpha * v + (1 - alpha) * rgb(bottom)[i]).toString(16).padStart(2, '0')).join('')}`;
// The two opacities in style.css that decide a contrast: the bars of the seat axis and the zoom hint over the map.
const BAR_OPACITY = Number(/\.chart \.bar \{[^}]*fill-opacity:\s*([\d.]+)/.exec(css)[1]);
const HINT_ALPHA = Number(/\.gesture \{[^}]*background:\s*rgba\(10, 12, 16, ([\d.]+)\)/.exec(css)[1]);

});
```

```js
// --- The vote axis of the House majority (Task 6.10) --------------------------------------------

export const VOTE_AXIS = { width: 560, height: 92, margin: { l: 14, r: 14, t: 52, b: 22 }, domain: [0.45, 0.6] };

/** Domain of the vote axis: about 45% to 60%, wider if a value lies outside. */
export function voteDomain(values, [low, high] = VOTE_AXIS.domain) {
  return [Math.min(low, ...values), Math.max(high, ...values)];
}

/** Ticks every 5 points inside the domain: [0.45, 0.6] gives 0.45, 0.5, 0.55, 0.6. */
export function voteTicks([low, high]) {
  const ticks = [];
  for (let k = Math.ceil(low * 20 - 1e-9); k <= Math.floor(high * 20 + 1e-9); k += 1) ticks.push(k / 20);
  return ticks;
}
```

```js
const plural = (n, one, many) => (n === 1 ? one : many);

/** National: the net change and the states whose seat count changed, from large R to large D. */
export function redrawView(results) {
  const n = results.national.redraw;
  const rows = Object.entries(results.states)
    .filter(([, s]) => s.redraw.change !== 0)
    .map(([postal, s]) => ({ postal, name: s.name, change: s.redraw.change, text: effectLine(s.redraw.change) }))
    .sort((a, b) => a.change - b.change || a.name.localeCompare(b.name, 'en'));
  return {
    sentence: `With these votes, the maps of the 2024 election give Democrats ${seats(n.seats_2024)} seats and the 2026 maps `
      + `${seats(results.national.enacted)}. Net change: ${effectLine(n.change)}.`,
    rows,
  };
}

/** In the sheet: "2024 map: 14 Democratic seats with these votes. 2026 map: 11 (3 fewer)." */
export function stateRedraw(state) {
  const r = state.redraw;
  if (!r.map_changed) return 'Map unchanged since 2024.';
  const difference = r.change === 0 ? 'the same number' : `${count(Math.abs(r.change))} ${r.change < 0 ? 'fewer' : 'more'}`;
  return `2024 map: ${seats(r.seats_2024)} Democratic ${plural(r.seats_2024, 'seat', 'seats')} with these votes. `
    + `2026 map: ${seats(state.enacted)} (${difference}).`;
}

/** The lines the extensions add to a state's sheet: under the heading, and after "Districts, sorted". */
export function stateExtras(results, postal) {
  const s = results.states[postal];
  return { head: [], body: [stateRedraw(s)] };
}
```

```js
export function senateView(results) {
  const s = results.national.senate;
  return {
    sentence: `If each state's two senators went to the party that won its presidential vote, Democrats would need `
      + `${share(s.share)} of the two-party vote for a Senate majority. They have ${share(s.base_share)} and would `
      + `win ${count(s.states_at_base)} ${plural(s.states_at_base, 'state', 'states')}.`,
    note: 'The Senate has no districts; the difference comes from each state having two senators, whatever its population.',
  };
}
```
